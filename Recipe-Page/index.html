Cajun Shrimp & Sausage Stuffed Cabbage

A healthly way to enjoy cabbage- with a flavorful twist of shrimp and sausage

Photo credit: HurryUpMeals

    For Cabbage

  • 2 medium green cabbages
  • 2 tbsp olive oil or butter
  • Salt & pepper, to taste
  • For the filling
  • 12oz smoked sauage(andouille or kielbasa), sliced
  • 1/2lb shrimp,peeled & deveined
  • 1/2 cup onion, chopped
  • 1/2 cup bell peppers(red & green), diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil or bacon fat
  • 1 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne (optional, for spice)
  • 1/2 cup shredded mozzarella or cheddar (optional, for extra gooeyness)

Instructions

Prepare the cabbage bowels:

  1. Preheat oven to 375ºF (190ºC)
  2. Slice off the tops of the cabbages and carefully core out the centers to make room for stuffing
  3. Rub insides with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Place in a baking dish.

Cook the filling

  1. In a skillet, heat olive oil or bacon fat
  2. Saute onion, pepperss, and garlic until softened
  3. Add sauage slices and cook until browned
  4. Toss in shrimp, season with Cajun seasoning, paprika, garlic powder, cayenne, salt & pepper
  5. Cook until shrimp turn pink (2-3 minutes)

Stuff the cabbagees

  1. Spoon shrimp & sauage mixture into each cabbage
  2. If using cheese, sprinkle inside before finishing with filling

Bake

  1. Cover dish loosely with foil. Bake for 40-50 mintues, until cabbage is tender
  2. Remove foil, bake an additional 10 minutes for a roasted finish

Serve:

  1. Garnish with parsley or green onions
  2. Slice wedges out of the cabbage bowls and enjoy hot
