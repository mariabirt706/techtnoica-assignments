Cajun Shrimp & Sauage Stuffed Cabbage

Healthly way to enjoy cabbage

      For cabbage:
      2 medium green cabbages
      2 tbsp olive oil or butter
      Salt 7 pepper, to taste
      For the filling:
      12oz smoked sauage(andouille or kielbasa), sliced
      1/2lb shrimp,peeled & deveined
      1/2 cup onion, chopped
      1/2 cup bell peppers(red & green), diced
      2 cloves garlic, minced
      2 tbsp olive oil or bacon fat
      1 tsp Cajun seasoning
      1/2 tsp paprika
      1/2 tsp garlic powder
      1/4 tsp cayenne (optional, for spice)
      1/2 cup shredded mozzarella or cheddar (optional, for extra gooeyness)
  • Instructions
  • Prepare the cabbage bowels:
  • Preheat oven to 375ºF (190ºC)
  • Slice off the tops of the cabbages and carefully core out the centers to make room for stuffing.
  • Rub insides with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Place in a baking dish.
